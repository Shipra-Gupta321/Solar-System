## **🌌Project Title:SolarView**
Explore the Wonders of Our Solar System! 🌞🪐<br>
This project is a fun and interactive web-based Solar System simulator. It shows all the planets, moons, and their orbits in a simple and visually appealing way. 🚀
With this project, you can see how the planets move around the Sun and get a clear idea of our cosmic neighborhood. Perfect for learning, exploring, or just enjoying a mini space adventure! 🌟
<br>

<hr>
## **Key Features:**

* **Realistic Planets:** Explore the solar system with beautifully designed planet models, showing accurate sizes, colors, and textures.<br>
* **Smooth Planet Orbits:** Watch planets move naturally around the Sun, following their real-life paths and speeds.<br>
* **Interactive Moons:** See moons orbit their planets, like Earth’s moon moving along its true elliptical path.<br>
* **Learn While You Explore:** Understand the solar system’s structure, the order of planets, and the distances between them with this educational visualization.
<br>

<hr>
## **Technologies Used:**

* **HTML5** – For structuring the content and layout of the Solar System project. 🧱<br>
* **CSS3** – For styling planets, orbits, and other visual elements. 🎨<br>
* **JavaScript** – For interactive features, animations, and orbital calculations. 🚀🌟
